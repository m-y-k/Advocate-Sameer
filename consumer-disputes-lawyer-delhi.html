Consumer Disputes Lawyer in Delhi

As a consumer, you have strong legal rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. Whether you have received a defective product, been a victim of poor service by a company, insurance company, hospital, bank, or builder — you are entitled to seek compensation and justice before the Consumer Forum without lengthy and expensive civil court proceedings. Adv. Sameer Khan provides expert consumer law representation before all consumer forums in Delhi.

Consumer Complaint Process

Consultation
Review your documents and assess whether a valid complaint exists
Send Legal Notice
Demand notice to the trader/service provider
File Complaint
Complaint filed before appropriate consumer forum with affidavit
Notice to Opposite Party
Forum issues notice; opposite party files reply
Hearings
Evidence, arguments from both sides
Order
Forum passes order awarding compensation, replacement or refund
Execution / Appeal
Order enforced or appeal filed if unsatisfied

Frequently Asked Questions

Any consumer who has purchased goods or availed services for personal use can file a complaint. A complaint can be filed against a trader, manufacturer or service provider for defective goods, deficiency in service, unfair trade practice, restrictive trade practice or overcharging. Consumer forums are accessible and do not require elaborate court procedure.
Consumer complaints in Delhi are filed based on the value of the claim: District Consumer Forum for claims up to Rs. 1 crore, State Consumer Commission for claims between Rs. 1 crore and Rs. 10 crore, and NCDRC (National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission) for claims above Rs. 10 crore. Appeals go from District to State to NCDRC to Supreme Court.
Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, consumer forums are required to decide cases within 3–5 months ideally. In practice, simpler cases at District Consumer Forums in Delhi are resolved in 6 months to 2 years. Cases with complex issues or multiple appeals can take longer.
Yes. Complaints against builders for defects in construction, delay in possession, failure to provide promised amenities and fraudulent misrepresentation can be filed before consumer forums. The NCDRC has frequently awarded heavy compensation against builders for deficiency in service. You can also approach RERA for real estate specific disputes.
