Property Dispute Lawyer in Delhi

Property disputes are among the most common and emotionally charged legal matters in Delhi. Disputes over ancestral property, encroachment by neighbours, fraudulent transfers, landlord-tenant conflicts and partition among heirs are handled daily in Delhi's courts. Adv. Sameer Khan has extensive experience in property law and civil litigation before all Delhi District Courts.

Property Disputes We Handle

  • Partition suits for division of jointly owned or ancestral property
  • Suit for declaration of title and ownership
  • Suit for possession of disputed property
  • Injunctions against illegal construction, encroachment and demolition
  • Cancellation of fraudulent sale deeds and transactions
  • Landlord-tenant disputes — eviction, rent recovery, restoration of possession
  • Disputes arising from DDA / builder allotment
  • Property disputes among co-heirs and legal heirs
  • Will disputes and succession matters

Common Types of Property Fraud in Delhi

  • Forged sale deeds using fake identity documents
  • Power of attorney misuse to transfer property without owner's consent
  • Double registration — same property sold to two different buyers
  • Benami transactions — property held in fictitious names
  • Fraudulent mutation of property records
  • Bogus will disputes to deprive legal heirs

Property Case Process in Delhi

Consultation

Legal Notice

File Suit
File civil suit in appropriate court with supporting documents
Interim Injunction
Apply for immediate stay/injunction to protect property
Trial
Evidence, examination of witnesses, expert reports
Judgment
Court passes decree for partition / possession / declaration
Execution
Decree is executed for actual delivery of possession

Frequently Asked Questions

A partition suit is filed to legally divide jointly owned property among co-owners — commonly among siblings after the death of a parent. It should be filed when the co-owners cannot agree on a mutually acceptable division. The court can pass a preliminary decree for partition and a final decree specifying each party's share.
You can file an application for a temporary injunction in the Civil Court to immediately stop the construction or encroachment, pending the final decision of the court. If the matter is urgent, courts can grant an ex-parte (one-sided) interim injunction on the same day. You should also file a complaint with the local municipal authority (MCD/NDMC/DDA).
A sale deed is the document executed at the time of sale that transfers ownership from seller to buyer. A title deed is a broader term for any document that evidences ownership — it includes sale deeds, gift deeds, will, inheritance certificates, and court decrees. A chain of valid title deeds establishes clear, marketable title to property.
Yes. Many property disputes — especially among family members — are successfully resolved through court-annexed mediation at Delhi's Mediation and Conciliation Centres. Mediation is faster, cheaper and preserves relationships. Our advocates can advise whether mediation is suitable for your dispute and represent you in the mediation proceedings.
